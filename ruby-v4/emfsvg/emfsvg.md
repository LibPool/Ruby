# emfsvg

**Tag**: filesystem

## 简介

Convert Microsoft Enhanced Metafile (EMF) to SVG and back. Consumes the `emf` gem's domain model. Replaces the FFI wrapper around libemf2svg with a pure-Ruby transformer.
