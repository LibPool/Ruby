# paynow_qr

**Tag**: template, tooling

## 简介

Zero-dependency pure-Ruby library to build PayNow (SGQR) QR payloads. Supports UEN and mobile proxies, fixed and editable amounts, reference numbers, and expiry dates. Pair with any QR rendering gem.
