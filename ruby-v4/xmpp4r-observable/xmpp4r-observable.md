# xmpp4r-observable

**Tag**: web, tooling

## 简介

Jabber::Observable builds on the strong foundation of XMPP4R and use a customized version of Ruby's builtin Observer pattern to expose XMPP observable things (as messages, pubsub events, presence, etc).
