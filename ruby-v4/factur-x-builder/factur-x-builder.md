# factur-x-builder

**Tag**: serialization, tooling

## 简介

Generates the EN 16931 factur-x.xml attachment of a Factur-X invoice from invoicing domain objects, with French e-invoicing identifiers (SIRET, SIREN, routing address) supported out of the box.
