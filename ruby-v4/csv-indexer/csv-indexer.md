# csv-indexer

**Tag**: web, testing, networking, filesystem, data

## 简介

CSV Indexer makes it simple the indexation and searching in lasge CSV files. It is not as robust as Lucence, but it is simple and cost-effective. May index files with millions of rows and find specific rows in matter of seconds. Find documentation here: https://github.com/leandrosardi/csv-indexer.
