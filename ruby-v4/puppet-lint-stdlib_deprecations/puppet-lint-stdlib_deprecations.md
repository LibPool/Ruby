# puppet-lint-stdlib_deprecations

**Tag**: tooling, data

## 简介

Helps to detect deprecated, removed and non-namespaced puppetlabs-stdlib functions and datatypes during your upgrade to puppetlabs-stdlib 9.x,
    and puppet 8.
