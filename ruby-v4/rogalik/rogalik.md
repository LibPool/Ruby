# rogalik

**Tag**: web, database, data

## 简介

Bring the power of AI to your Rails app without the boilerplate. Rogalik seamlessly synchronizes your Active Record models with vector databases (like pgvector), providing an idiomatic, convention-driven API for generating LLM embeddings and powering Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
