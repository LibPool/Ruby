# em-synchrony-moped

**Tag**: library

## 简介

EM-Synchrony-Moped is a Moped driver patch for EM-Synchtony, allowing your asynchronous application use non-blocking connections to MongoDB.  Moped is the MongoDB driver for the Mongoid ORM.
