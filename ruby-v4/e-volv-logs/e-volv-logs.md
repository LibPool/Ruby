# e-volv-logs

**Tag**: web, networking

## 简介

Feature flags evaluated locally against e-volv Launch, plus batched log shipping, trace context and error capture for e-volv Observer. Pure Ruby + stdlib; Rack middleware, Rails.logger bridge, ActiveJob and Sidekiq carriers, Net::HTTP and Faraday injection.
