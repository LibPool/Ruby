# responds_to_parent2

**Tag**: web, filesystem

## 简介

[Rails] Adds 'responds_to_parent' to your controller to respond to the parent document of your page.Make Ajaxy file uploads by posting the form to a hidden iframe, and respond with RJS to the parent window.
