# petrovich

**Tag**: library

## 简介

A morphological library for Russian anthroponyms, such as first names, last names, and middle names.

## 官网

- 主页: https://petrovich.nlpub.ru/
- 源码仓库: https://github.com/petrovich/petrovich-ruby
- 文档: https://nlpub.ru/Petrovich
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/petrovich
