# evvnt-submission-form-angular-rails

**Tag**: web

## 简介

evvnt-submission-form-angular packaged for Rails assets pipeline. This gem is designed to make life easier when integrating the Evvnt submission form into rails apps - but it's only a wrapper around the angular module, saving you from having to include the source or use bower/npm when you don't have to
