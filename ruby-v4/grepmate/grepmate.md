# grepmate

**Tag**: web, template

## 简介

Extremely fast search of rails projects or rails source for code, open in textmate or browser with html output

## 官网

- 主页: http://github.com/ssoroka/grepmate
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/grepmate

## 历史版本号

- 2.0.5 (2009-10-16)

## 获取地址

- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/grepmate

- Bundler: `gem "grepmate"`
