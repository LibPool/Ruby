# cattify

**Tag**: tooling

## 简介

An English-to-cat language translator analogue to Dogeify in "Guide to Building Your First Ruby Gem Tutorial" by Matt Huggins. This is simply a practice in making my first Ruby Gem, but it is great for making your Ruby application comprehensible to felines.
