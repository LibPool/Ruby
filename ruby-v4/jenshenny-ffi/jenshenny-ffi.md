# jenshenny-ffi

**Tag**: testing

## 简介

TEST gem - not for production. The FAT (all Ruby ABI slices) counterpart of jenshenny-ffi 1.17.4 (arm64-darwin), published alongside the CA skinny version to test that the resolver defaults to the skinny CA tuple on Ruby 4.0. Yanked after the experiment.
