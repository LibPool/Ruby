# flexmock-minitest

**Tag**: testing

## 简介

Some lame monkeypatches to make a small subset of FlexMock functionality work
with MiniTest::Unit and MiniTest::Spec.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/phiggins/flexmock-minitest
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/flexmock-minitest
