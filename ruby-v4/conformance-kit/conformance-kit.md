# conformance-kit

**Tag**: testing

## 简介

Shared RSpec examples, a per-Ractor storage shim, a class-state audit, RuboCop configs and a reusable CI workflow that hold a gem to explicit thread-safe, fiber-safe and Ractor-clean tiers.
