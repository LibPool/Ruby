# rails_2_preload

**Tag**: web, testing

## 简介

The purpose of Rails2Preload is to optimize testing with Spin
 and Rails 2. It does this by splitting the Rails 2 initialization method
 (Rails::Initializer#process) into two phases: preload and postload.
