# mkv

**Tag**: data

## 简介

Simple wrapper around MKVToolNix's mkvinfo utility to get data from MKV movies, and mkvextract to extract subtitles.

## 官网

- 主页: http://github.com/gokuu/mkv
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/mkv
