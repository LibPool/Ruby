# bookworm_buddy

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Bookworm Buddy is a gem that allows you to choose from 50 Barnes &amp; Noble book categories to see the top 20 bestselling books in that category. You can then choose a book from the list to see a short description. Happy reading!
