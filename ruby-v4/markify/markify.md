# markify

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Markify is a ruby script to detect new marks or course assessments in the student
information system of your university. If markify detects new marks, they will send you a XMPP message for
every change. Supported universities: University of Applied Sciences Bonn-Rhein-Sieg.
