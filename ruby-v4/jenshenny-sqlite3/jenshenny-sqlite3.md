# jenshenny-sqlite3

**Tag**: database, testing

## 简介

TEST gem - not for production. The FAT (all Ruby ABI slices) counterpart of jenshenny-sqlite3 2.9.6 (arm64-darwin), published alongside the CA skinny version to test that the resolver defaults to the skinny CA tuple on Ruby 4.0. Yanked after the experiment.
