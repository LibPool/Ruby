# adminpage_scaffold_generator

**Tag**: web, networking, template, tooling, filesystem

## 简介

You can generate template files easy and beautiful by this scaffold generator for Ruby on Rails. These templates are based on http://www.webresourcesdepot.com/free-admin-template-for-web-applications/
