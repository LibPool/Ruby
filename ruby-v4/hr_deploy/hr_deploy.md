# hr_deploy

**Tag**: web, database, devops, data

## 简介

Easily deploy your Rails apps to Heroku with one command, automatically taking care of things such as checking current Heroku status, enabling maintenance mode, creating database backups, etc. Multiple deployment targets are supported
