# sidekiq_solid_fetch

**Tag**: library

## 简介

A reliable fetch strategy for Sidekiq that prevents job loss on worker crashes by atomically moving jobs to per-worker processing queues and recovering orphaned jobs from dead workers. An open-source alternative to Sidekiq Pro's `super_fetch`.
