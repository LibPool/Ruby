# lilt

**Tag**: web, tooling, data

## 简介

Lilt provides a table-driven lexer, a Pratt (top-down operator precedence) parser engine extended with juxtaposition and multiple expression tables, position tracking, and an s-expression reader and printer. Grammars are plain data; ASTs are whatever your handlers build.
