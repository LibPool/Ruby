# gn0m30-uuid

**Tag**: web, networking, tooling

## 简介

UUID generator for producing universally unique identifiers based on RFC 4122
(http://www.ietf.org/rfc/rfc4122.txt).

- 主页: http://github.com/gn0m30/uuid
