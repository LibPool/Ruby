# gpxpose

**Tag**: filesystem

## 简介

A simple library to read your GPX files and draw them onto OSM map tiles.

## 官网

- 主页: http://github.com/ellmo/GPXpose
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/gpxpose
