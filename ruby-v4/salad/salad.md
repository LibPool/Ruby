# salad

**Tag**: web, cli, testing, filesystem

## 简介

salad bundles the SpeciesFileGroup Ruby API wrappers (bananomia, bell_pepper, bok_choy, checkerberry, colrapi, crawlyflower, hookkaido, nasturtium, plazucchini, syconium, wikimelon) and ships a `salad` executable that launches a Pry console with all of them pre-loaded and available under both their service names (Salad.inaturalist) and their gem names (Salad.nasturtium).
