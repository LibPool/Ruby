# mistri

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Mistri (مستری) is the fixer: an agent harness that lives inside your app. Durable sessions in your own store, streaming, tools, fire-and-forget human approval, steering, compaction, structured output, skills, and sub-agents, across Anthropic, OpenAI, and Gemini, with zero runtime dependencies.
