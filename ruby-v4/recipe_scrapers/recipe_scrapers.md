# recipe_scrapers

**Tag**: serialization, template, data

## 简介

Reads the title, ingredients, steps, times, yields and nutrition of a recipe from its page. Uses the schema.org JSON-LD or microdata the site publishes, falls back to OpenGraph, and takes a short per-site declaration where a site publishes no structured data. Fetches through a Faraday connection guarded against SSRF, or parses HTML you already have.
