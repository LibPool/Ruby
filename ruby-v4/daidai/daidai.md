# daidai

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Daidai is a table-driven, pure-Ruby port of jconj's conjugation algorithm, built on the JMdictDB conjugation tables. It conjugates Japanese verbs and adjectives from a dictionary-form word and its JMdict part-of-speech code, with no native extension and nothing to download at runtime.
