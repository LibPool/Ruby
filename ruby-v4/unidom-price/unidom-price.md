# unidom-price

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Unidom (UNIfied Domain Object Model) is a series of domain model engines. The Price domain model engine includes Pricing and its relative models. Unidom (统一领域对象模型)是一系列的领域模型引擎。价格领域模型引擎包括定价及其相关的模型。
