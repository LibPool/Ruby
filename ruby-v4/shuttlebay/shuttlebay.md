# shuttlebay

**Tag**: web, networking

## 简介

Shuttlebay serves a Rack app (Rails, Sinatra, Roda) as a Mothership
[[bays.http]] bay. Mothership owns HTTP; a native engine turns docking
protocol frames into Rack calls on preforked, threaded workers.
