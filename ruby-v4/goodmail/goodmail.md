# goodmail

**Tag**: web, cli, template

## 简介

Send beautiful, simple transactional emails with zero HTML hell. Goodmail is a minimal, opinionated, expressive Ruby DSL for sending production-grade transactional emails in Rails apps that look good in any email client out of the box.
