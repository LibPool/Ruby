# nbp-rates

**Tag**: library

## 简介

A library that download currency rates for PLN (polish zloty).

## 官网

- 主页: http://www.nbp.pl
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/nbp-rates

## 历史版本号

- 0.1.0 (2010-06-06)

- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/nbp-rates

- Bundler: `gem "nbp-rates"`
- 最新版本: 0.1.0
- 最新版归档: https://rubygems.org/downloads/nbp-rates-0.1.0.gem
