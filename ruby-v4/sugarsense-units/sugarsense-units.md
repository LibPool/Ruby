# sugarsense-units

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Glucose helpers extracted from the Sugar Sense CGM monitoring app: mg/dL to mmol/L conversion, trend arrow codes, glucose zone classification, time in range distribution and the GMI formula. Informational only, contains no dosing logic.
