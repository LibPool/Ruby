# bynn-decisionnode

**Tag**: web, cli

## 简介

A typed client for DecisionNode, a decision engine: ask truth, choice, score and number questions about any state, its images and its videos, and get calibrated answers back, with image uploads, sessions, the Batch API, retries, typed errors and the safety check result.
