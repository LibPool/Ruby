# mcpeye

**Tag**: web

## 简介

mcpeye captures what your agents try to do through your MCP tools — including the asks your tools could NOT fulfill — and ships them to a self-hosted mcpeye instance for the Intent Gap Report. This gem instruments a Ruby/Rails MCP server.
