# activerecord-turbopuffer-adapter

**Tag**: library

## 简介

An unofficial, community-maintained Active Record adapter for turbopuffer: vector and full-text ranking, filters, batch writes and aggregates through the familiar ActiveRecord interface. Not affiliated with turbopuffer.
