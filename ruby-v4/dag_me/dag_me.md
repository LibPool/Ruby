# dag_me

**Tag**: cli, database

## 简介

Directed Acyclic Graph Management Engine for ActiveRecord. Edges as source of truth, transitive closure maintained by PL/pgSQL triggers. Cycle rejection, ancestors/descendants, topological ordering. PostgreSQL 18+ only.
