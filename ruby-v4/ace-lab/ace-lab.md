# ace-lab

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Lab environment package: read-only topology registry and capability router (stable project, agent, and service IDs from ADR-022 configuration), the lab status snapshot, and ace-lab prepare — idempotent preparation of accounts, directories, permissions, ed25519 keys, and role bindings from a root-owned install manifest. No queue, journal, or command kernel.
