# mongo

**Tag**: database, data

## 简介

A pure-Ruby driver for connecting to, querying, and manipulating MongoDB
databases. Officially developed and supported by MongoDB, with love for
the Ruby community.

## 官网

- 主页: https://mongodb.com/docs/ruby-driver/
- 源码仓库: https://github.com/mongodb/mongo-ruby-driver
- 文档: https://mongodb.com/docs/ruby-driver/current/tutorials/quick-start/
- 更新日志: https://github.com/mongodb/mongo-ruby-driver/releases
- 问题追踪: https://jira.mongodb.org/projects/RUBY
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/mongo
