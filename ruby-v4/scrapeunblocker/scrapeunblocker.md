# scrapeunblocker

**Tag**: web, serialization, template, devops, data

## 简介

JS-rendered pages that bypass Cloudflare, DataDome, PerimeterX and Akamai, plus Google SERP and Skyscanner flights/hotels/car-hire scraping as JSON.
