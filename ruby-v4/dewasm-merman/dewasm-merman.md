# dewasm-merman

**Tag**: cli, template

## 简介

The merman Mermaid renderer (Rust) compiled to wasm and converted to pure Ruby by dewasm.
It renders Mermaid text to SVG or to terminal text without a browser, a native extension, or a wasm runtime.
