# ptero

**Tag**: web, database, template, tooling, data

## 简介

Ruby on Rails-style MVC framework for PHP. Builds fast and clean projects in seconds with pure-PHP controllers, templates, and models. RESTful routes, modular templates, databases without a drop of SQL. You won't be disappointed. Download this gem, then run `$ ptero new &lt;appname&gt;` and be on your merry way.
