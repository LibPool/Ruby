# ssh4iot

**Tag**: web, networking

## 简介

Easy way to configure and register reverse ssh tunnels for iot devices by keysharing over https to manage and enjoy

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/mpantel/ssh4iot
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/ssh4iot
