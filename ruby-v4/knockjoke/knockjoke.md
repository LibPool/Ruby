# knockjoke

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Currently in development. knockjoke is an app that collects jokes over the internet. Our goal is to collect them all and give visitors an oppotunity to decide which are really funny and which are not. All KnockJoke visitors can change joke rating. On knockjoke.com you'll find only decent jokes. No rude, diry or offensive jokes. If you find such jokes please don't hesitate to contact us and we will remove it.
