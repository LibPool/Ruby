# coat-of-many-colors

**Tag**: template

## 简介

Agents leave scraps everywhere: logs, diffs, notes, results. coat collects
them, records where each one came from at the moment it was made, and
stitches them into a single self-contained HTML page. Provenance lives in
an append-only hash-chained ledger, every source keeps its own patch color,
and anywhere two agents covered the same ground is marked as a seam, open
until a person says it is settled. Wires itself into Claude Code's hooks so
a session collects itself.
