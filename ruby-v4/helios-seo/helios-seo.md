# helios-seo

**Tag**: web, serialization, template, data

## 简介

A content-agnostic Rails engine that renders SEO metadata, Open Graph/Twitter cards, and a JSON-LD graph for any resource matching a small duck-typed contract, plus a site-level /llms.txt.
