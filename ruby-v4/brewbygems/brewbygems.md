# brewbygems

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Adds RubyGems post-install and post-uninstall hooks to update Homebrew bin/ symlinks

## 官网

- 主页: http://github.com/indirect/brewbygems
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/brewbygems
