# gregoryfoster-gchartrb

**Tag**: web, networking

## 简介

gchartrb is a Ruby wrapper around the Google Chart API, located at http://code.google.com/apis/chart/. Visit http://code.google.com/p/gchartrb to track development regarding gchartrb.
