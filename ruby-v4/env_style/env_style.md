# env_style

**Tag**: web

## 简介

EnvStyle is a mountable Rails engine that tints your app's favicon a distinct color per environment at runtime, so side-by-side browser tabs are visually distinguishable. It adds a route and a <link> tag to your layout, works with any front-end (Hotwire, Inertia, or plain Rails), and leaves production untouched.
