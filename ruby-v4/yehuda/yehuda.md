# yehuda

**Tag**: tooling

## 简介

Named after the dreamer who revived the hebrew language, yehuda was created out of my necessity of acquiring hebrew vocabulary. If you are also trying to master this wonderful language, this gem might help you to build the tools that will enrich your learning experience.
