# flunky

**Tag**: cli

## 简介

Flunky gives AI agents a real browser: a swappable driver, a page-to-prompt snapshot, a Capybara-style action DSL, and vendor-neutral tool schemas plus a dispatcher. The gem never calls an AI vendor itself; model clients are injected.
