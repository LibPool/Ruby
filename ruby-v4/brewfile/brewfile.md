# brewfile

**Tag**: filesystem

## 简介

Generates a Homebrew Brewfile from currently installed formula for easy backup

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/jakewmeyer/Brewfile-Generator
- 文档: https://www.rubydoc.info/gems/brewfile/1.0.0
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/brewfile

- 1.0.0 (2018-03-04)

## 获取地址

- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/brewfile

- Bundler: `gem "brewfile"`

- 最新版归档: https://rubygems.org/downloads/brewfile-1.0.0.gem
