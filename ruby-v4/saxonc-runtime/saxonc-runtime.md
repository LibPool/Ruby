# saxonc-runtime

**Tag**: filesystem

## 简介

A small helper gem that downloads and exposes SaxonC binaries for the `saxonc` gem (or any consumer that needs `SAXONC_HOME`). It detects your platform, fetches the right archive, and keeps it in a cache.
