# m2x

**Tag**: networking, devops, data

## 简介

AT&amp;T’s M2X is a cloud-based fully managed data storage service for network connected machine-to-machine (M2M) devices. From trucks and turbines to vending machines and freight containers, M2X enables the devices that power your business to connect and share valuable data.
