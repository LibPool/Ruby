# number_normalizer

**Tag**: library

## 简介

In free texts, numbers maybe writen as digits (eg. 123.4) or words (eg. one hundred fifty) or a mixed way (eg. 1 million). This gem will help you to normalize or extract the numbers presented in the free text.
