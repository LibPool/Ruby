# JimNeath-fudge-form

**Tag**: web, tooling

## 简介

Fudge Form is a way of building easier, semantically correct forms using a custom form builder. Building forms in rails is boring and repetitive. This plugin helps to remove the boredom and fills you full of form building mega powers.
