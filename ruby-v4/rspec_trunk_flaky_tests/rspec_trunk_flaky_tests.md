# rspec_trunk_flaky_tests

**Tag**: testing, data

## 简介

Integrates RSpec with Trunk Flaky Tests to automatically upload test results from your CI jobs. Enables accurate flaky test detection, quarantining, and analytics.

## 官网

- 主页: https://docs.trunk.io/flaky-tests/get-started/frameworks/rspec
- 源码仓库: https://github.com/trunk-io/analytics-cli/tree/main/rspec-trunk-flaky-tests
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/rspec_trunk_flaky_tests
