# linkeddata

**Tag**: serialization, data

## 简介

A metadistribution of RDF.rb including a full set of parsing/serialization plugins.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/ruby-rdf/linkeddata

- 问题追踪: https://github.com/ruby-rdf/rdf/issues
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/linkeddata
