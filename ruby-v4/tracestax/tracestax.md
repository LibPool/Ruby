# tracestax

**Tag**: web

## 简介

Worker intelligence and observability for Ruby background jobs. Supports Sidekiq (6.x/7.x), Solid Queue (Rails 8), Resque, Delayed::Job, and Good Job. Automatic instrumentation of job lifecycle, queue health, and anomaly detection.
