# graphql_declarative

**Tag**: networking

## 简介

Declare filtering, sorting, cursor pagination, and preloading on a graphql-ruby resolver instead of hand-writing them per endpoint. Pagination stays correct through association filters, and preloading is derived from the query's selection set.
