# jruby-launcher

**Tag**: tooling

## 简介

Builds and installs a native launcher for JRuby on your system

## 官网

- 主页: http://jruby.org
- 文档: https://www.rubydoc.info/gems/jruby-launcher/2.0.2
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/jruby-launcher
