# protovalidate

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Ruby implementation of protovalidate. Validates Protobuf messages against the buf.validate rules in their schema, evaluated with CEL by protovalidate-cc through a native extension. Precompiled gems are published for Linux, macOS and Windows.
