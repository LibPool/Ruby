# celly

**Tag**: web, data

## 简介

This gem provides an easy-to-use DSL for retrieving player and team statistics from the National Hockey League's API. Simple methods calls will give access to a player's regular-season or playoffs statistics by season or for an entire career, roster information for each team, league standings, and franchise data.
