# administrate-column_filters

**Tag**: tooling

## 简介

Builds a dashboard's COLLECTION_FILTERS from its ATTRIBUTE_TYPES: substring and exact matching for text columns, comparisons and ranges for numbers and dates, and association ids, instead of one hand-written lambda per column.
