# xades

**Tag**: security, serialization, filesystem

## 简介

Sign and verify XML documents with XAdES-BES / BASELINE-B advanced electronic signatures (ETSI EN 319 132), the profile used for eIDAS-adjacent flows such as Poland's KSeF e-invoicing authentication. Pure MRI: Nokogiri for XML/C14N, OpenSSL for RSA/ECDSA.
