# vanillicons

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Vanillicons are a small generated avatar made by vanillaforums.org, they use a md5 hash as the image key. Images can be generated on the site at vanillicon.com. The images come in 3 different sizes; 50px, 100px and 200px.
