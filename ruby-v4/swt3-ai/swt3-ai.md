# swt3-ai

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Mint, verify, and sign SWT3 witness anchors for AI compliance. EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, CMMC, SR 11-7. Cross-language parity with Python and TypeScript SDKs. Zero external dependencies.
