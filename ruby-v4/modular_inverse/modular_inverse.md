# modular_inverse

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Given two integers ‘a’ and ‘m’, find modular multiplicative inverse of ‘a’ under modulo ‘m’.
                          The modular multiplicative inverse is an integer ‘x’ such that.
                          a x ≡ 1 (mod m)  and x belongs to [0,1,2, .. m-1]
