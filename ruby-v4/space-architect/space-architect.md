# space-architect

**Tag**: cli, serialization, filesystem

## 简介

A dry-cli CLI for spaces: date-prefixed directories with a YAML identity file, $PWD-based current-space resolution, and XDG config/state. Provisions repos at copy-on-write speed from evergreen checkouts, concurrently on fibers. Ships fish shell integration and completions.
