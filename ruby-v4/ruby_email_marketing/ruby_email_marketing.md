# ruby_email_marketing

**Tag**: web, cli

## 简介

A Ruby gem for managing and sending marketing emails. This gem provides an easy-to-use interface to create email campaigns, handle mailing lists, and track metrics such as open rates and clicks. Ideal for small businesses and individuals who need a straightforward, code-driven solution for their email marketing needs.
