# capistrano-magento2

**Tag**: web, testing, devops

## 简介

A Capistrano extension for Magento 2 deployments. Takes care of specific Magento 2 requirements and adds tasks specific to the Magento 2 application.

- 主页: https://github.com/davidalger/capistrano-magento2
- 文档: https://www.rubydoc.info/gems/capistrano-magento2/0.9.4
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/capistrano-magento2
