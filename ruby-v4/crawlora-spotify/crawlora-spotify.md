# crawlora-spotify

**Tag**: web, cli, filesystem

## 简介

Ruby client for Crawlora's hosted Spotify API. Covers search, tracks, albums, artists, playlists, profiles, audiobooks, charts, and podcast shows and episodes across 38 endpoints.
