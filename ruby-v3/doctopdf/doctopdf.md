# doctopdf

**Tag**: web, template

## 简介

Jruby gem to convert microsoft office documents to pdf. Useful to render office documents in browser in rails web app settings.

## 官网

- 主页: http://github.com/gauravgaur111/doctopdf
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/doctopdf
