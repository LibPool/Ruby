# dm-delayed-job

**Tag**: data

## 简介

Delated_job (or DJ) encapsulates the common pattern of asynchronously executing longer tasks in the background. It is port to DataMapper of direct extraction from Shopify where the job table is responsible for a multitude of core tasks.
