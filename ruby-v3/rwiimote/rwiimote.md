# rwiimote

**Tag**: data

## 简介

Control and get data from your wiimote with ruby. Wrapper around libcwiimote.

## 官网

- 主页: http://www.rikiji.it
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/rwiimote

## 历史版本号

- 0.0.1 (2010-05-09)

## 获取地址

- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/rwiimote

- Bundler: `gem "rwiimote"`

- 最新版归档: https://rubygems.org/downloads/rwiimote-0.0.1.gem
