# zip-codes-pl

**Tag**: tooling, filesystem, data

## 简介

Builds and refreshes a local dataset of Polish postal codes (PNA), including locality, voivodeship, county and commune TERYT codes, and coordinates. PNA and coordinates come from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0), while administrative names come from Poczta Polska.
