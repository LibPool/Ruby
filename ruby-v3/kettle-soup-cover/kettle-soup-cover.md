# kettle-soup-cover

**Tag**: testing

## 简介

🥘 A Covered Kettle of Test Coverage SOUP (Software of Unknown Provenance)
Four-line SimpleCov config, w/ curated, opinionated, pre-configured, dependencies
for every CI platform, batteries included.
