# free-scrape

**Tag**: web, database, data

## 简介

A web-scraping interface to freebase.com, the open and shared database of the world's knowledge.

## 官网

- 主页: http://freebase.rubyforge.org/
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/free-scrape
