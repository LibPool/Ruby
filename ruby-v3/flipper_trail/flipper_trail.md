# flipper_trail

**Tag**: devops

## 简介

FlipperTrail records an append-only audit trail of Flipper feature-flag changes via an adapter decorator: the acting user, the operation, and the before/after state. Storage backends for ActiveRecord and Mongoid. Not affiliated with Flipper or Flipper Cloud.
