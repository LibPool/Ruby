# rcommonsmath

**Tag**: library

## 简介

A thin jruby wrapper for commons-math (mainly just a way to load the jars in a structured way for now)

## 官网

- 主页: http://rubygems.org/gems/rcommonsmath
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/rcommonsmath
