# carboy

**Tag**: tooling, filesystem

## 简介

When building homebrew formulae, there's a process needed to build and deliver them. Add this to your rakefile and you'll automatically get the standard rake targets needed to deliver. These targets expect a standard directory structure and two collections to drive the process.
