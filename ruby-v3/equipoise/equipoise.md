# equipoise

**Tag**: web, security, serialization

## 简介

A thin, dependency-free transport for syncing a producer's people into an Equipoise account's CRM as Contacts. All business logic lives server-side; this gem handles auth, JSON, bounded retry/backoff, idempotency, and typed errors.
