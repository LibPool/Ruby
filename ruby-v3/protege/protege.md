# protege

**Tag**: web, networking

## 简介

Protege gives your Rails app AI agents: inbound mail via Action Mailbox, replies over SMTP, tools that act on your own models, cron-scheduled responsibilities dispatched through Active Job, and a built-in dashboard to manage and observe it all — in process, no separate service.
