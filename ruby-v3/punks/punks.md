# punks

**Tag**: library

## 简介

punks - generate your own 24×24 (or 32×32 or 12×12) punk (incl. Matt & John's® ye olde punk v1/v2 anno 2017 style), phunk, philip, marilyn, marc, saudi/sheik, man in black, hottie 'n' more (pixel) avatar / character images (off chain) from text attributes (via built-in spritesheets); incl. 2x/4x/8x zoom for bigger sizes and more
