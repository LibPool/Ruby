# argus-trail

**Tag**: security, template, tooling

## 简介

Plug-and-play role/permission management with a unified audit log of role reassignments and permission grants/revokes, shipped as a mountable engine with paginated HTML admin screens and generators for install/views/config. Agnostic to auth (works standalone, auto-integrates with Pundit) and pagination (auto-integrates with Kaminari).
