# active_record_postgres_recovery

**Tag**: database, networking, devops

## 简介

Recovers from stale ActiveRecord PostgreSQL connections after AWS RDS or Aurora failover, deploys, restarts, and network interruptions by retrying safe reads, clearing pools, and reporting recovery events.
