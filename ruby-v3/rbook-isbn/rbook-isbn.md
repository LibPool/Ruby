# rbook-isbn

**Tag**: library

## 简介

This library is designed to make working with both 10 and 13 digit ISBNs easier.

## 官网

- 主页: http://rbook.rubyforge.org/
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/rbook-isbn

## 历史版本号

- 1.0 (2009-07-25)

## 获取地址

- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/rbook-isbn

- Bundler: `gem "rbook-isbn"`
- 最新版本: 1.0
- 最新版归档: https://rubygems.org/downloads/rbook-isbn-1.0.gem
