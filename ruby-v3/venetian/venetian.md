# venetian

**Tag**: testing

## 简介

Bundles the Playwright driver binary in platform-specific gem variants and integrates with `capybara-playwright-driver`.

## 官网

- 主页: https://malcic.codes/projects/software/venetian
- 源码仓库: https://github.com/jmalcic/venetian
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/venetian
