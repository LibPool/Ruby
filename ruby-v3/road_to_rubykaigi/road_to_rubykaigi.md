# road_to_rubykaigi

**Tag**: cli

## 简介

Road to RubyKaigi is a Ruby gem that delivers a nostalgic, text-based action game experience. Dodge obstacles, overcome bugs, and beat the deadline to reach RubyKaigi. All in your terminal.
