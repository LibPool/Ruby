# xcframework_converter

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Convert an ancient .framework (dynamic or static) to an .xcframework. Add an arm64 Simulator patch.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/igor-makarov/XCFrameworkConverter
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/xcframework_converter
