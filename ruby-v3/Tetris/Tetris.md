# Tetris

**Tag**: library

## 简介

A simple Tetris game created as a semestral project for Programming in Ruby course at FIT CTU. Use your Space key to rotate shapes, left and right arrows to move shapes and down arrow to speed up falling. The game automatically alters the speed with relation to your score.
