# mysigner

**Tag**: testing, tooling, filesystem

## 简介

Command-line interface for managing iOS certificates, devices, provisioning profiles, and Android keystores. Build, sign, and upload to App Store Connect and Google Play with simple commands like 'mysigner ship testflight' and 'mysigner ship internal --platform android'.
