# herringbone

**Tag**: filesystem

## 简介

Reads and writes Apache Parquet files in Ruby, without Thrift or native extensions of its own. Snappy and LZ4 are implemented in Ruby and GZIP uses zlib; ZSTD and Brotli are available when the zstd-ruby / brotli gems are installed.
