# librollenspielsache-rb

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Complete Ruby-idiomatic wrapper for librollenspielsache.  WIP - there's very little to the underlying binding, and as such, very little here.  Version numbers for both should remain in sync, and this won't be "usable" until 1.0.
