# ealdent-resque-scheduler

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Light weight job scheduling on top of Resque.
    Adds methods enqueue_at/enqueue_in to schedule jobs in the future.
    Also supports queueing jobs on a fixed, cron-like schedule.
