# prime_methods

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Adds to Integer class: trial_factor, is_prp?(b=2), is_square?, jacobi_symbol, minimal_strong_jacobi(a=0), is_fu?, is_bpsw2?, next_prime, factor, factorize
