# bundler-skills

**Tag**: library

## 简介

A gem that discovers skills/ directories bundled in your dependency gems and symlinks them into your project's agent skill directories (.claude/skills, .agents/skills) via the `bundle exec skills` command. The Ruby/Bundler counterpart of antfu/skills-npm.
