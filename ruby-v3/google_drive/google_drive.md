# google_drive

**Tag**: filesystem

## 简介

A library to read/write files/spreadsheets in Google Drive/Docs.

- 文档: https://www.rubydoc.info/gems/google_drive/3.0.7
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/google_drive
