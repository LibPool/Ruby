# qr_reader

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Detects and decodes QR codes in photos and scanned documents (PNG, JPEG, JPEG 2000, GIF, BMP, PNM, TIFF incl. CCITT fax, PDF incl. vector-drawn codes) without native extensions or external tools.
