# bridgetown-sasso

**Tag**: tooling

## 简介

A Bridgetown plugin that compiles your Sass/SCSS entrypoints with sasso, a pure-Rust, zero-dependency, byte-for-byte dart-sass alternative. Compilation runs in-process during the build (no Node toolchain, no Dart VM, no subprocess).
