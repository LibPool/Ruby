# google-genai

**Tag**: cli

## 简介

This gem is an unofficial port of the official Google Python SDK, providing a Ruby interface for developers to integrate Google's generative models, including the Gemini family, into their applications. This port was primarily developed with the assistance of the Gemini CLI.
