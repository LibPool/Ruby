# fillform

**Tag**: web, template, devops

## 简介

Fills AcroForm templates in pure Ruby and draws the filled fields with the layout of pdftk (iText 2.1.7): no Java, no pdftk binary, no subprocess. Ships a PdfForms-compatible API.
