# localmail

**Tag**: web, database, data

## 简介

Localmail swaps ActionMailer's delivery for the mailer actions you name, stores the message in your database or Redis, and serves it from a mountable inbox. It works where mailcatcher and letter_opener_web cannot: across dynos and containers that share no disk.
