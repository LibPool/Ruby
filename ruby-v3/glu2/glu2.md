# glu2

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Glu bindings for the opengl gem, split into a separate gem because of Glu deprecation.

## 官网

- 文档: https://www.rubydoc.info/gems/glu2/8.4.0
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/glu2

## 历史版本号

- 8.4.0 (2024-09-08)

## 获取地址

- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/glu2

- Bundler: `gem "glu2"`

- 最新版归档: https://rubygems.org/downloads/glu2-8.4.0.gem
