# juxtaparssionate

**Tag**: library

## 简介

juxtaparssionate parses and compares job postings from various popular job boards. By comparing word frequency, sentence length, and parts-of-speech counts, juxtaparssionate produces metrics loosely determining the quality and uniqueness of a job posting
