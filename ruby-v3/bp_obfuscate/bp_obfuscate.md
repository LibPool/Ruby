# bp_obfuscate

**Tag**: web, security

## 简介

A small wrapper around OpenSSL for encrypting and obfuscating short values such as ids in URLs. Derives its key from a Rails application's secret_key_base, or from ENV["SECRET_KEY_BASE"] outside of Rails.
