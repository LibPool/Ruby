# lex-prospection

**Tag**: web, testing

## 简介

Future-oriented mental simulation: imagines future scenarios, predicts their emotional impact, tracks impact bias and focalism corrections, and calibrates forecast accuracy over time. Based on Gilbert & Wilson affective forecasting research.
