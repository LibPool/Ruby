# stimulus-components

**Tag**: web, cli

## 简介

A collection of over 25 Stimulus controllers packaged as a Rails gem.
Includes controllers for clipboard, dropdown, dialog, carousel, and more.
Designed for easy integration with Rails 8 and modern asset pipelines.
