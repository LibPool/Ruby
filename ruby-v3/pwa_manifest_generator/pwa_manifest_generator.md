# pwa_manifest_generator

**Tag**: web, networking, tooling

## 简介

This gem helps you generate, validate, and manage Progressive Web App (PWA) manifests in your Rails application, with advanced configuration, validation, and generator support. See https://github.com/Dandush03/pwa_manifest_generator for details.
