# evil_faker

**Tag**: database, tooling

## 简介

EvilFaker gives you a curated corpus of adversarial strings (emoji, RTL overrides,
zalgo, SQL/script injection, unicode edge cases) as a Faker-style generator, plus
an audit that feeds them through your ActiveRecord models and reports what crashes,
what gets silently corrupted, and what round-trips cleanly.
