# zen_garden

**Tag**: web, testing, tooling, devops

## 简介

ZenGarden is a continuous deployment workflow for Rails apps. After testing your app on Travis-CI, it deploys your app to Heroku upon successful builds. ZenGarden is built with Rake tasks for extreme ease.
