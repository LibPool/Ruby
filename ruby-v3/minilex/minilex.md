# minilex

**Tag**: tooling

## 简介

A little lexer toolkit, designed for the cases where parsers do the parsing and lexers do the lexing.

- 主页: http://github.com/satchmorun/minilex
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/minilex
