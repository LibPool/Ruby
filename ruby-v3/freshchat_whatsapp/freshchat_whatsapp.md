## 简介

The Ruby gem support for Freshchat WhatsApp integration enables seamless communication by leveraging the power of Freshchat within Ruby applications. It allows developers to integrate WhatsApp messaging functionalities into their Ruby-based projects using Freshchat's comprehensive features.
