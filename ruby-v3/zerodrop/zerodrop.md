# zerodrop

**Tag**: testing, devops

## 简介

ZeroDrop catches verification emails at Cloudflare's edge and auto-extracts OTP codes and magic links. Test email verification, magic links, OTP and password reset flows in RSpec, Minitest or Capybara — no regex, no Docker, no signup.
