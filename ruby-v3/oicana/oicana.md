# oicana

**Tag**: template

## 简介

Generate typeset PDFs in Ruby from Typst templates, in process. No headless browser, no per-document fees.

## 官网

- 主页: https://oicana.com
- 源码仓库: https://github.com/oicana/oicana/tree/main/integrations/ruby
- 文档: https://oicana.com/docs
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/oicana
