# listenable

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Listenable makes it easy to wire ActiveRecord models to listener classes. Define <Model>Listener classes in app/listeners, declare listen :on_created, :on_updated, etc., and Listenable automatically injects callbacks and subscribes to ActiveSupport::Notifications.
