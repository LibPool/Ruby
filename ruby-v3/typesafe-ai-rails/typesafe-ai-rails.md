# typesafe-ai-rails

**Tag**: web

## 简介

Rails configuration, model-aware call/cost telemetry, and fail-closed confidence policies for TypeSafe Choice and Score judgments on top of the community typesafe-sdk Ruby gem. This is not an official TypeSafe package.
