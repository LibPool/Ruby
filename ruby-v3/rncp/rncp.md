# rncp

**Tag**: filesystem

## 简介

rncp provides a fast way of transfering files between systems.
Its pure Ruby port of Pyncp/Ncp.

## 官网

- 主页: http://github.com/jecxjo/rncp
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/rncp
