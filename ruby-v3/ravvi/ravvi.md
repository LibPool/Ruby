# ravvi

**Tag**: testing, tooling

## 简介

Companion utilities for Ravvi, an AI ad generator: canvas specs, aspect ratios and UI-safe areas for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn and X ad placements.
