# fontico

**Tag**: template, tooling

## 简介

Declare every icon in your app in one manifest — from Lucide, Material Symbols, any Iconify set, or your own SVG folder — under names you choose. fontico resolves, normalises and merges them into a single build artifact, so templates never name a vendor.
