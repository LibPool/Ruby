# bpci

**Tag**: web, networking

## 简介

*** bpci is a *fork* of cijoe (https://github.com/defunkt/cijoe/). ***

  cijoe is a sinatra-based continuous integration server. It's like an
  old rusty pickup truck: it might be smelly and gross, but it gets the
  job done.
