# repolho

**Tag**: template, data

## 简介

Repolho generates HTML reports from .rep.erb templates with support for nested groupings, page breaks, headers/footers, and statistics (sum, avg, min, max). Works with ActiveRecord::Result, Relation, or arrays of hashes.
