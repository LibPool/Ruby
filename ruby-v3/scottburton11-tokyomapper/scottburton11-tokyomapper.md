# scottburton11-tokyomapper

**Tag**: library

## 简介

TokyoMapper is a simple ORM for TokyoCabinet. Just 'include TokyoMapper' in your class and go! Requires the tokyocabinet-ruby bindings and the tokyocabinet package.
