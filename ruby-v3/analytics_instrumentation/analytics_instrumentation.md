# analytics_instrumentation

**Tag**: filesystem, data

## 简介

Add analytics to any app, quickly, robustly, and accurately. 

Define events and their properties in a single, simple .yml file.

Plus: Get valuable campaign attribution, and best-practices learned across dozens of projects, to streamline the collection of clean, usable, reliable product and user data.
