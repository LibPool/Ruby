# guard-mirror

**Tag**: template, filesystem

## 简介

A CoffeeScript, Stylus, and Jade (HTML and JST) Guard that mirrors
    yoursource files (.coffee/.styl/.jade/.jst.jadet) in another location
    (public/www/etc...).
