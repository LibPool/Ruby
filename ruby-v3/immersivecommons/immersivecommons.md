# immersivecommons

**Tag**: web, cli, testing, tooling

## 简介

Official Ruby client for Immersive Commons (Floor 10 of Frontier Tower, a members-run AI builder space in San Francisco). Table-driven from the published OpenAPI spec: events & RSVPs, members directory, resource booking, research Q&A, and RFC 8628 device-code token minting. Zero runtime dependencies.
