# gifd

**Tag**: cli

## 简介

Allows users to maintain a library of gif locations, and easily copy those to their clipboard/open them in their browser.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/indspenceable/gifd
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/gifd

## 历史版本号

- 0.0.3 (2012-08-16)
- 0.0.1 (2012-08-11)
