# vtracer

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Wrapper of rust VTracer library, converter of raster images (like jpg & png) into vector graphics

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/OlegSavinov/vtracer
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/vtracer

## 历史版本号

- 0.1.0 (2025-11-16)

## 获取地址

- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/vtracer

- Bundler: `gem "vtracer"`

- 最新版归档: https://rubygems.org/downloads/vtracer-0.1.0.gem
