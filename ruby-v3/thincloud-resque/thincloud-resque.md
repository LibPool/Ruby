# thincloud-resque

**Tag**: web, devops

## 简介

Thincloud Resque is an extraction of the New Leaders resque recipes packaged up as a Rails engine to manage dependencies and configuration.

## 官网

- 主页: http://newleaders.github.com/thincloud-resque
