# tuscan

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Converts from from temperature sensor's electrical signal to temperature and vice-versa.
Allowable sensors: ITS-90 compliant SPRT's, IEC 60751 compliant PRT's and IEC 60584 compliant thermocouples.
