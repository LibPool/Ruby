# sekki24

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Sekki24 calculates the 24 solar terms, 72 microseasons, supplementary
seasonal observances, new moons, and Japanese lunisolar dates for years
1900 through 2100 using pure Ruby and no runtime dependencies.
