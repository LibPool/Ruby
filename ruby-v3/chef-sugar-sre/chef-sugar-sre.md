# chef-sugar-sre

**Tag**: library

## 简介

A series of helpful sugar of the Chef core and other resources to make a cleaner, more lean recipe DSL, enforce DRY principles, and make writing Chef recipes an awesome experience! [Forked by Combell SRE for Chef 16]
