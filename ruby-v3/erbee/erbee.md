# erbee

**Tag**: web, database, data

## 简介

Erbee is a Ruby gem designed to automatically generate Entity-Relationship (ER) diagrams for your Rails applications. Leveraging the power of Mermaid for visualization, Erbee provides an easy and flexible way to visualize your database schema and model associations directly from your Rails models.
