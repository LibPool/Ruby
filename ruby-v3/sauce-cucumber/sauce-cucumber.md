# sauce-cucumber

**Tag**: library

## 简介

A Cucumber driver to use Sauce Labs with Cucumber and Capybara.  Supports job naming, job success updates and more!  [DEPRECATED] Sauce Labs does not recommend this gem. It will not receive further development or support.
