# spree_uber_direct

**Tag**: web

## 简介

Dispatches completed Spree orders as Uber Direct deliveries — live delivery-fee quoting during checkout, dispatch on order completion, and delivery status synced back via webhooks. A second, independent delivery provider alongside spree_doordash — same shipping-method-based routing pattern, offered as an additional choice, not a replacement.
