# rubydraw

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Rubydraw is a high level drawing/game library,
    like Gosu or Rubygame.
    Dependancies: ruby-sdl-ffi, SDL (not a ruby gem),
    and 2DArray.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/awostenberg/rubydraw
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/rubydraw
