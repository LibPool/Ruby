# triplepat-syntheticalert

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Provides a time-based callback to drive a synthetic alert metric. Register it as a gauge, alert on the gauge, and route the alert to a Triple Pat check-in timer; if the check-ins stop, your alerting is broken.
