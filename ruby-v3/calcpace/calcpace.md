# calcpace

**Tag**: web

## 简介

Ruby gem for runners: pace, time, and distance calculations, unit conversions (30+ units), race time predictions (Riegel & Cameron), GPS track analysis (Haversine, elevation gain, per-km splits), age grading (WMA 2023), VO2max estimation (Daniels & Gilbert), and personalized training zones (Daniels paces & Karvonen heart-rate zones).
