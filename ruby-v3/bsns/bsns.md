# bsns

**Tag**: serialization

## 简介

Static YAML assets which live in your application repositories.  For the streamlined modification of informational, promotional and business assets by non-technical personnel, providing project stake-holders with an unparalleled ability to efficiently allocate resources and expedite engineering man hours.
