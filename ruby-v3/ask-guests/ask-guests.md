# ask-guests

**Tag**: web

## 简介

Give anonymous visitors a durable identity with metered AI access: signed guest sessions, per-day turn/token budgets, single-use claiming of records into a real owner on sign-up, and stale-session retention. Framework-agnostic core with optional ActiveRecord and Rails adapters.
