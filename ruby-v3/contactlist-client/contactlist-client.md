# contactlist-client

**Tag**: cli

## 简介

The contactlist-client gem is a ruby client to contactlist service which retrieves contact list of email(hotmail, gmail, yahoo, sohu, sina, 163, 126, tom, yeah, 189 and 139) and im(msn)
