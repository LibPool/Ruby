# db2

**Tag**: web, data

## 简介

Rails Driver and Adapter for IBM Data Servers: {DB2 on Linux/Unix/Windows, DB2 on zOS, DB2 on i5/OS, Informix (IDS)}

## 官网

- 主页: http://rubyforge.org/projects/rubyibm/
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/db2
