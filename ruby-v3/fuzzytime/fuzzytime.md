# fuzzytime

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Sample output:
    2:30AM => half past 2
    7:50AM => 10 til 8
    12:00PM => noon
    5:15PM => quarter past 5
    9:00PM => 9 o'clock
    10:17PM => 17 past 10
    12:00AM => midnight
