# goblins

**Tag**: library

## 简介

goblins - generate your own 24×24 goblins or 20×20 lil'goblins (pixel) monster avatar images (off-blockchain) from text attributes (via built-in spritesheets); incl. 2x/4x/8x zoom for bigger sizes and more
