# crawlora-google

**Tag**: web, cli, template

## 简介

Ruby client for Crawlora's hosted Google API. Covers Maps search, places, reviews and photos; News; Trends; Finance quotes and markets; suggestions; videos; and jobs across 40 endpoints.
