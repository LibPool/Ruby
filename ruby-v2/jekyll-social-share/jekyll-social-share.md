# jekyll-social-share

**Tag**: template

## 简介

Adds a {% social_share %} Liquid tag that renders share buttons for Bluesky, Mastodon, Facebook, Reddit, LinkedIn, X, Threads, email, and copy-link. Icons are inline SVGs from Simple Icons (CC0). Fully configurable via _config.yml.
