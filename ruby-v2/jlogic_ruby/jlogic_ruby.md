# jlogic_ruby

**Tag**: web, serialization, networking, tooling

## 简介

Build complex rules, serialize them as JSON, and execute them in ruby. Maintained fork of json_logic with fixes for nil/== handling, if, reduce, in, === range matching, and the transform_keys core monkey-patch. See http://jsonlogic.com
