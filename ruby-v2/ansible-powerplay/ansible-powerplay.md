# ansible-powerplay

**Tag**: testing

## 简介

Ansible Powerplay, by way of its DSL, allows you to
                       specify your Ansible playbooks and their vars, and common
                       vars to all, so that you can run your
                       indeoendent playbooks in full parallel.
