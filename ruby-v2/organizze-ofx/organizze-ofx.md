# organizze-ofx

**Tag**: tooling

## 简介

A simple OFX (Open Financial Exchange) parser built on top of Nokogiri.
Currently supports OFX 102, 200 and 211.

Usage:

  OFX("sample.ofx") do |ofx|
    p ofx
  end
