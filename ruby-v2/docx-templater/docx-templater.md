# docx-templater

**Tag**: serialization, template, filesystem

## 简介

PURE RUBY GEM (works on Windows)! Can work with:
- $SOME_VALUE$;
- $REPEAT:SOME_COLLECTION$ (at least, new row) $EACH:SOME_VALUE_OF_COLLECTION_ITEM$ (at least, new row) $UNREPEAT:SOME_COLLECTION$;
Repeating commands must be at the separate paragraphs.
Note, that created template may look good but have bad structure for templater, so you should open docx as a zip archive, open "word/document.xml" and edit it
