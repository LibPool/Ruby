# jekyll-asset-pipeline

**Tag**: tooling, filesystem

## 简介

Adds asset preprocessing (CoffeeScript, Sass, Less, ERB, etc.) and asset compression/minification/gzip (Yahoo YUI Compressor, Google Closure Compiler, etc.) to Jekyll.
