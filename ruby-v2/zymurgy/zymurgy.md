# zymurgy

**Tag**: library

## 简介

(n) The branch of chemistry that deals with fermentation processes, as in brewing.

Zymurgy is a collection of libraries to help with beer brewing calculations:
        * Gravity points
        * Hop utilisation
        * Boil times and volumes
