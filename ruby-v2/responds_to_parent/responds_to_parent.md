# responds_to_parent

**Tag**: web, filesystem

## 简介

[Rails] Adds 'responds_to_parent' to your controller torespond to the parent document of your page.Make Ajaxy file uploads by posting the form to a hiddeniframe, and respond with RJS to the parent window.
