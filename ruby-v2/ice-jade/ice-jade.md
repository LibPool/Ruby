# ice-jade

**Tag**: web, cli, serialization, networking

## 简介

A lightweight Ruby SDK featuring Quantum (IM robot messaging), Getter & Poster (zero-dependency HTTP GET & POST clients with JSON/Form/Multipart support and sharing a common IceJade::Transport layer).
