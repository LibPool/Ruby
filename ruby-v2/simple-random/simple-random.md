# simple-random

**Tag**: tooling

## 简介

Simple Random Number Generator including Beta, Cauchy, Chi square, Exponential, Gamma, Inverse Gamma, Laplace (double exponential), Normal, Student t, Uniform, and Weibull.  Ported from John D. Cook's C# Code.
