# newspeakie

**Tag**: web, networking

## 简介

Newspeak is the language of Oceania, a fictional totalitarian state ruled by the Party, who created the language to meet the ideological requirements of English Socialism (Ingsoc). You can use it to create dummy text for your web app using Faker modules, or write your own language -- a "Duckspeak" in Newspeak parlance and then "Whistle" to get "quacks" using a simple url format: https://newspeak.ie/lorem/sentence.txt
