# mobgpsd

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Turns your smartphone in a gpsd device (think wardriving)

## 官网

- 主页: http://github.com/nofxx/mobgpsd
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/mobgpsd

## 历史版本号

- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/mobgpsd
