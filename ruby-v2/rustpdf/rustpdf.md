# rustpdf

**Tag**: web, security, networking, template

## 简介

Generate, edit, sign and process PDFs in Ruby: PDF/A, AES-256 encryption, digital signatures (PAdES), forms, stamping, redaction, text extraction and page rendering. Docs: https://rustpdf.dev/docs/ruby

## 官网

- 文档: https://www.rubydoc.info/gems/rustpdf/0.5.0
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/rustpdf
