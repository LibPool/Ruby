# ipgeolocation

**Tag**: web, testing

## 简介

Remote, IP-Based Geolocation for everyone!  Seriously, this gem gives you 3 different IP-location services as 1-liners. Example:  IPGeolocation.locate(&quot;12.345.678.90&quot;, :blogama)  That's all there is to it! And you get a bunch of information, especially from blogama's API.
