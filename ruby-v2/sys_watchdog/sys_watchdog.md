# sys_watchdog

**Tag**: web, networking

## 简介

sys_watchdog keeps your *NIX servers green by performing periodic checks and optionally actions like service restarts and notifications. https://github.com/tomlobato/sys_watchdog http://sys-watchdog.bettercall.io.
