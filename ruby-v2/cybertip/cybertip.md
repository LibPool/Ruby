# cybertip

**Tag**: testing

## 简介

Cybertip gem is created to assist CyberTipLine, which is operated in partnership with the FBI,
                          Immigration and Customs Enforcement, U.S. Postal Inspection Service, U.S. Secret Service, 
                          military criminal investigative organizations, U.S. Department of Justice, Internet Crimes 
                          Against Children Task Force program, as well as other state and local law enforcement agencies.
                          Reports to the CyberTipline are made by the public and Electronic Service Providers. 
                          ESPs are required by law to report apparent child pornography to law enforcement via the 
                          CyberTipline (18 U.S.C. § 2258A).
