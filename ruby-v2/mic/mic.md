# mic

**Tag**: testing

## 简介

Market Identifier Codes (MIC). The ISO 10383 specifies a universal method of identifying exchanges, trading platforms, regulated or non-regulated markets and trade reporting facilities as sources of prices and related information in order to facilitate automated processing.
