# haml_ejs

**Tag**: web, cli, template

## 简介

Haml+ejs is an extension to haml to make generating client-side templates
      as simple as server-side markup.

## 官网

- 主页: http://haml-lang.com/
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/haml_ejs
