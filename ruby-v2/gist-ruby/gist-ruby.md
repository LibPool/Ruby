# gist-ruby

**Tag**: web

## 简介

Gist is a customer communication and marketing automation platform that helps you grow your business and engage with your customers across their lifecycle.
                          It is a complete marketing and growth stack for your business - with live chat, email marketing, automation, event tracking and lead gen forms
