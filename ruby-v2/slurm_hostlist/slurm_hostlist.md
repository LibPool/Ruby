# slurm_hostlist

**Tag**: testing

## 简介

Pure Ruby, dependency-free expansion and compression of Slurm hostlist
expressions like "n[01-16],login[1-2]". A port of Slurm's hostlist.c,
differentially tested against Slurm to give the same results as
`scontrol show hostnames`, `show hostlist` and `show hostlistsorted`.
