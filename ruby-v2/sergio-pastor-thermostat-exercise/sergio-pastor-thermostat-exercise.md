# sergio-pastor-thermostat-exercise

**Tag**: serialization, template

## 简介

Application to control the temperature (Thermostat), convert
                  temperatures (fahrenheit,kelvin and celisus) and print them on
                  the screen with different formats (Json,Html and Text).
