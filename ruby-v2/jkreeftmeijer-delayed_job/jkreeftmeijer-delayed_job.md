# jkreeftmeijer-delayed_job

**Tag**: library

## 简介

A fork of Zachary Belzer's fork (which added MongoMapper support) of Tobias Lütke's DelayedJob. Delayed_job (or DJ) encapsulates the common pattern of asynchronously executing longer tasks in the background.
