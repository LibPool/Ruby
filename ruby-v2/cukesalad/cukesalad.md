# cukesalad

**Tag**: library

## 简介

CukeSalad allows you to focus on the tasks at hand - expressing examples, the roles involved in those examples and the tasks that those roles need to perform with the product under development.
  New in this version:
    - Single or double quotes can be used for parameters
    - Single or double quotes can be used for expectations
    - You can initialise the actor by having a role_preparation method in your role
