# silence_rails_3_2_plugin_deprecations

**Tag**: web, filesystem

## 简介

Turn off Rails 3.2 deprecation warnings for plugins in vendor/plugins.  The goal is to put in the nondevelopment groups in the gemfile to silence the warnings.
