# time_pup

**Tag**: tooling

## 简介

A simple natural language date time parser extracted from hound.cc. Perfect for parsing the local part of an email address

## 官网

- 主页: http://www.hound.cc
- 源码仓库: http://www.github.com/siyelo/time_pup
