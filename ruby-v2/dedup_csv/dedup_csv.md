# dedup_csv

**Tag**: filesystem, data

## 简介

Given 2 CSV files, remove all rows from the second CSV that are present in the first CSV.

## 官网

- 主页: http://github.com/kingsleyh/dedup_csv
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/dedup_csv
