# irtoy

**Tag**: web, networking

## 简介

A gem for transmitting and receiving infrared signals from an IR Toy. Heavily inspiered by https://github.com/crleblanc/PyIrToy and https://github.com/djellemah/irtoy-hexbug-spider .
