# acv2lrtemplate

**Tag**: template, filesystem

## 简介

Easily convert .acv (Photoshop Curves) to .lrtemplate (Adobe Lightroom Preset) files.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/laurentzziu/acv2lrtemplate
- 文档: https://www.rubydoc.info/gems/acv2lrtemplate/0.2.1
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/acv2lrtemplate
