# accounts

**Tag**: web, filesystem

## 简介

Accounts::Server defines the following paths for your web-app:

* POST '/logon'
* POST '/register'
* POST '/forgot-password'
* POST '/change-password'
* POST '/change-email'

Your app must provide the pages and forms that will post to these paths.
