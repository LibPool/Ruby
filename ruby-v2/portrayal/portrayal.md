# portrayal

**Tag**: tooling

## 简介

Inspired by dry-initializer and virtus, portrayal is a minimalist gem that takes a somewhat different approach to building struct-like classes. It steps away from types, coersion, and writer methods in favor of encouraging well-designed constructors. Read more in the Philosophy section of the README.
