# gem_radar

**Tag**: web, filesystem

## 简介

Reads a project's Gemfile.lock and reports, for each direct gem, whether it's compatible with the project's Ruby/Rails, needs a mandatory update, is deprecated or archived, or can be upgraded safely vs. with breaking changes.
