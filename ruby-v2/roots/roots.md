# roots

**Tag**: library

## 简介

For val (real/complex) and root n: val.root(n,[1-n]) and val.roots(n, [opt]); For Integer ival: ival.iroot2, and ival.iroot(n) or ival.irootn(n); Roots.digits_to_show to see/change number of digts to show
