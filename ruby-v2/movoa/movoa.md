# movoa

**Tag**: web, cli, tooling

## 简介

Unlimited AI video generator with sound: on paid plans the tenth take of a shot costs what the first did, and your first clip is free to make right now. A zero-dependency Ruby client for Movoa (movoa.ai): text to video and photo to video with sound, on MiniMax H3, Seedance 2.5, Seedance 2.0 and Veo 3.1 Fast, with one API key.
