# rubysip

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Ruby/SIP is library of SIP(RFC3261 and more) written in pure ruby

## 官网

- 主页: http://rubysip.rubyforge.org/
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/rubysip
