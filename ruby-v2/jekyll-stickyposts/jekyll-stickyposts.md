# jekyll-stickyposts

**Tag**: library

## 简介

This Jekyll plugin sticks/pins posts tagged sticky: true, i.e. moves them before all others. Optionally, the posts could also appear where they normally would. Sorting and ordering options are available. Compatible with pagination and collections.
