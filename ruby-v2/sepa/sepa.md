# sepa

**Tag**: serialization

## 简介

Generate ISO20022 XML messages. Implements a subset of pain.008.001.02 and pain.008.001.04 CustomerDirectDebitInitiation for now. WARNING: NO WARRANTY, USE AT YOUR OWN RISK AND PERIL.
