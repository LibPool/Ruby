# days_picker

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Days Picker helps you find the total Weekdays, Sundays, Mondays, Tuesdays, Wednesdays, Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays of a Month or in a Year. (ie Weekdays.in_jan(2012), Sundays.in_jan(2012) ,Saturdays.by_year(2012) .. )
