# vagrant-guest-qnx

**Tag**: library

## 简介

A plugin to allow vagrant to recognize instances of QNX Neutrino RTOS. 

Guest capabilities are limited to what I can find from other providers and map to QNX.

Currently:

 * halt
 * change_hostname
