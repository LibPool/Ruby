# redux-gem

**Tag**: web, networking, tooling

## 简介

Adds a component and container generator to better manage your projects and creates an easy system to re-use code in between projects. This gem sits on top of React_on_Rails and still has all of their functionality
	full read me at: https://github.com/Luke-Popwell/redux-rails-gem
