# patinfo2csv

**Tag**: testing, serialization, data

## 简介

Patinfo2csv extracts and converts patinfo.yaml to patinfo.csv;
only 4 chapters(de, text) in patinfo.yaml are extracted.

* effects
* contra_indications
* pregnancy
* usage
* composition

You can specify the eancode, see `patinfo2csv --help`
