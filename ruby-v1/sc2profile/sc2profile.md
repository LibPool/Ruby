# sc2profile

**Tag**: filesystem

## 简介

Scrapes name, 1v1 league and 1v1 league points from a Battle.net StarCraft II profile.

## 官网

- 主页: http://github.com/MatthieuSegret/sc2profile
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/sc2profile
