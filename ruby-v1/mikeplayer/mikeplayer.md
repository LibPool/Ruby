# mikeplayer

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Wraps Sox's `play` command, allowing playslists, find, random and time limit.
Once a song is playing you can:
'x' to previous
'c' to pause/play
'v' to next
'q' to quit
't' n to set a timer that will pause the music after n minutes
