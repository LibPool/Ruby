# autojenkins

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Library to remotely control Jenkins CI. Autojenkins can fetch, create, enable/disable and launch jobs in Jenkins

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/neurogeek/autojenkins-rb
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/autojenkins
