# cocupu

**Tag**: cli, data

## 简介

Cocupu is a platform for collecting, curating and publishing your data.  This client allows you to interface with that data.

## 官网

- 主页: http://cocupu.com
- 源码仓库: http://github.com/cocupu/client
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/cocupu
