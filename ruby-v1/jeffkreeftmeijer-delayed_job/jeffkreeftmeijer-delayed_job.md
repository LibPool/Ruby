# jeffkreeftmeijer-delayed_job

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Delated_job (or DJ) encapsulates the common pattern of asynchronously executing longer tasks in the background. It is a direct extraction from Shopify where the job table is responsible for a multitude of core tasks. This is a fork of Zachary Belzer's fork of Tobias Lütke's DelayedJob.
