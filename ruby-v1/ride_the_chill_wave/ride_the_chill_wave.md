# ride_the_chill_wave

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Helps you chill out when coding Ruby. Require 'chill' and then call .chill? or .is_chill? on any of your Ruby objects to find out if they are chill or not (spoiler: they probably are).
                   

 Version 1.0.1: Now even BasicObjects can be chill!
                   
 Version 1.0.2: Unfortunately, certain people are not chill.
                   
 Version 1.0.3: With time, chill status can be regained.
                   
 Version 2.0.0: Now your puts statements are more chill with the addition of chill colors.
                   
 Version 2.0.1: Fixed a totally not chill bug
