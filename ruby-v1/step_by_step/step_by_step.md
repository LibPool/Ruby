# step_by_step

**Tag**: web, networking

## 简介

Alternative to https://github.com/FetLife/rollout, with an Active Record backend and additional helpers, partially based on Ryan Bates's custom solution in http://railscasts.com/episodes/315-rollout-and-degrade.
