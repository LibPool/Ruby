# puppet-lint

**Tag**: tooling

## 简介

Checks your Puppet manifests against the Puppetlabs style guide and alerts you to any discrepancies.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/puppetlabs/puppet-lint/
- 文档: https://www.rubydoc.info/gems/puppet-lint/5.1.1
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/puppet-lint
