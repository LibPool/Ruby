# jsonapi_mapper

**Tag**: web, serialization

## 简介

Sanitizes a jsonapi Document and maps it to ActiveRecord,
    creating or updating as needed.
    Prevents mistakes when assigingng attributes or referring to 
    unscoped relationships.
