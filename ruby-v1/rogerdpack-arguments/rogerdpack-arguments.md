# rogerdpack-arguments

**Tag**: testing

## 简介

You don't have to wait until Ruby 2.0 to get (named|keyword) arguments support. Arguments has been tested with Ruby 1.8.6 and ruby 1.9.1 and eventually will work with JRuby
