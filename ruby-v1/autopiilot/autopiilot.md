# autopiilot

**Tag**: web, data

## 简介

AutoPIIlot simplifies GDPR and privacy compliance in Rails
    apps by automatically handling sensitive data annotations, retention policies,
    audit logging, and privacy endpoints directly at the ORM and logging layers.
    It provides instant compliance with minimal effort, ensuring data privacy is
    effortless and maintainable.
