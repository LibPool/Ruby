# onigiri

**Tag**: library

## 简介

This gem is supposed to replace a tidy-ext in one of our projects. Tidy-ext has nasty memory leaks, tends to crash and is incompatible with Ruby 1.9. So here I am trying to use a japanese saw to make some rice balls.
