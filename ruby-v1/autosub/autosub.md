# autosub

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Ruby tool to automatically download subtitles (srt) inside your TV Shows folder

## 官网

- 主页: http://github.com/pirate/autosub
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/autosub

## 历史版本号

- 0.4.0 (2009-11-06)
