# dig_bang

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Ruby 2.3 introduces the new Hash#dig method for safe extraction of a nested value. It is the equivalent of a safely repeated Hash#[] or Array#[]. #dig! on the other hand, is a repeated #fetch.
