# gepub

**Tag**: tooling

## 简介

gepub is a generic EPUB parser/generator. Generates and parse EPUB2 and EPUB3

## 官网

- 主页: http://github.com/skoji/gepub
- 文档: https://www.rubydoc.info/gems/gepub/2.0.1
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/gepub
