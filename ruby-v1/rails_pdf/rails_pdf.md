# rails_pdf

**Tag**: web, template, filesystem

## 简介

Reliable way to generate PDF files of any complexity. Support HTML/ERB/CSS/SCSS/PUG/Javascript/ChartsJS/Images/SVG/Custom Fonts/etc.
