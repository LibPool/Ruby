# humanoid

**Tag**: library

## 简介

A mirror of Mongoid (an awesome Ruby ODM framework for MongoDB) with a name I can say out loud.

## 官网

- 主页: http://mongoid.org
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/humanoid

## 历史版本号

- 1.2.7 (2010-02-21)

- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/humanoid

- 最新版归档: https://rubygems.org/downloads/humanoid-1.2.7.gem
