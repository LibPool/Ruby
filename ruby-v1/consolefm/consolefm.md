# consolefm

**Tag**: cli

## 简介

A gem that communicates with console.fm and returns information on all the popular songs on the site for any sub-genre of electronic music

- 主页: https://github.com/ArtemTitoulenko/consolefm
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/consolefm
