# hat-trick

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Hat-Trick brings together jQuery, validation_group, and Ajax to make multi-step forms awesome. It tries to insulate you from this complexity by providing a simple yet powerful DSL for defining the steps of your form and their behavior.
