# hassless

**Tag**: filesystem

## 简介

Makes LESS less of a hassle on read-only filesystems by compiling and serving it up for you.

## 官网

- 主页: http://github.com/objectreload/hassless
- RubyGems: https://rubygems.org/gems/hassless
