# culqi

**Tag**: web, security

## 简介

This gem allows your Ruby (1.9.2+) or Rails (3.2+) app to quickly integrate with Culqi Payment processor.
                          It handles Culqi's flavor of encryption, decryption and Culqi ticket creation.
