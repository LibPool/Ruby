# bh

**Tag**: tooling

## 简介

A form builder that dresses every field a page asks for, the components Bootstrap ships behavior for and no markup — a combobox, a 6-digit code, a dialog, a toast, a thread of messages — and the one stylesheet and script that carry them, served by the engine.
