# urushiol

**Tag**: testing

## 简介

Urushiol was born out of necessity. When we decided to migrate our reverse proxy routing from Apache to Varnish we noticed that this would not be a trivial matter. As the migration came to a halt due to unexpected routing errors we decided that, having a proper way of testing our configs would not be a bad idea. Urushiol is the product of that idea.
