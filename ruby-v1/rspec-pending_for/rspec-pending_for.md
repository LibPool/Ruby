# rspec-pending_for

**Tag**: testing

## 简介

⏳️ Mark specs pending or skipped for specific Ruby engine (e.g. MRI or JRuby) & versions, or version ranges. Fund overlooked open source projects - bottom of stack, dev/test dependencies: floss-funding.dev
